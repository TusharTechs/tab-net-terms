Airwallex Agentic Banking Hackathon · Northwind Data's credit desk

AI agents can buy. Now they can buy on credit, and the supplier still gets every dollar.

Businesses run on trade credit: net-30, 2% off for paying early. Agents get none of it: they prepay by card. TAB is the supplier's credit desk for agent buyers. Claude runs the agents and reads the messy text; code decides every price, limit, credit and collection; Metal, Visa via Airi, Airwallex and T:0 move and record the money.

Everything on this page is one live run, : Metal testnet, the Airwallex and T:0 sandboxes, real Claude calls and one passkey-approved Visa hold. Each Metal transaction links to the public explorer.

01The gap

Agents prepay

An AI buyer has a card and a budget, so it pays up front, every time. Its company's cash is tied up and the early-pay discounts finance teams live on never happen.

Suppliers can't say yes

Extending terms means trusting the buyer to pay later. With software buyers there is no credit file, no signature, and no way to collect if it doesn't.

Nothing enforces the terms

A promise in a chat log is not a payment. Credit needs limits a human signed, collections the buyer can't dodge, and books that tie out.

03Claude proposes, code decides

Claude does the reading

  • Buyer agents choose what to buy and how to pay, through typed tools
  • Compiles the CFO's plain-English goals into proposed policy changes
  • Reads a dispute email into claims
  • Says what it can't express (net-90, "until month end") instead of guessing

Code does the deciding

  • Prices, limits and terms in integer cents
  • Hard rails on every policy change, rejected never clamped; a signature the desk verifies itself
  • Credits only for units the supplier's own QA flagged, at the price charged
  • Collections only through mandates the buyer's human signed

Labelled, not hidden

  • Globex's and Initech's books are a stand-in; Acme's are real T:0 sandbox records.
  • Airwallex deposits are sandbox simulations; quotes, conversions and card payments are real sandbox calls.
  • Due dates are compressed to minutes so the run fits a video; stale units are set by the script, standing in for delivery QA.
